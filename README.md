\# 🌱 EcoRescue



\### AI-Powered Surplus Food Rescue Network



EcoRescue is an AI-powered prototype that helps prevent surplus food from becoming waste by connecting food donors with receivers who can rescue and redistribute it.



\## 🚨 Problem



Large amounts of edible surplus food can be wasted from homes, restaurants, events, hostels and other places while nearby people or organizations may need food.



EcoRescue provides a simple digital workflow to help rescue that surplus food.



\## 💡 Solution



A donor can:



1\. Upload a photo of surplus food.

2\. Provide basic food and pickup information.

3\. Let Amazon Nova Pro analyze the food image.

4\. Receive an AI-generated food category, rescue priority and recommended action.

5\. Post the donation for receivers.



A receiver can:



1\. View available food donations.

2\. See the AI analysis and pickup information.

3\. Accept a donation for rescue.



The Impact Dashboard tracks rescued donations and servings.



\## 🤖 AI Feature



EcoRescue uses Amazon Bedrock with Amazon Nova Pro.



Nova Pro analyzes food images and provides:



\- Food identification

\- Food category

\- Rescue priority

\- Recommended rescue action

\- Food safety reminder



The AI is used as decision support and does not guarantee food safety.
