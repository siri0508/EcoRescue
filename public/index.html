🔄 How EcoRescue Works

A simple four-step food rescue workflow.

1

📷 Upload

A donor uploads a photo of their surplus food.

2

🤖 Analyze

Amazon Nova Pro analyzes the food image and provides a rescue recommendation.

3

🤝 Match

The donation becomes available for a receiver to accept.

4

🌱 Rescue

A receiver accepts the donation and the rescued food contributes to the impact dashboard.

🤖 Powered by Amazon Nova Pro

EcoRescue uses Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Nova Pro's multimodal AI capabilities to analyze food images, identify the food category, assign a rescue priority and recommend a practical rescue action.

AI provides decision support; donors and receivers remain responsible for confirming appropriate food handling and storage.
